MOVIES

I enjoy...

There are many types of entertainment TV, theatre, music, books, and more. My favorite of all of them is movies. So much work goes into movies that make them feel like one long extensive mix of artforms. Some of these artforms include the well thought out writing, the engineering in sets, the skillful acting, and more. All those talented people that work on movies put all their skills together to make one long immersive film for viewers to enjoy.

My fav movies

I have some criteria on what makes a movie good. One thing that makes a good movie for me is a well written well thought out story. A story that makes me think, a story that leaves me amazed the second I walk out of the theatre. Although the biggest thing I like about a movie is when a movie makes me feel something, whether it's anger, sadness, compassion, or anything in between. What movies do I love that fit my criteria?

John Wick
I enjoy John wick because it provides a heartfelt revenge story while also giving long accelerating action sequences.
interstellar
